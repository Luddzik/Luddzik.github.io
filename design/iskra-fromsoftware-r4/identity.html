ISKRA / IDENTITY PROPOSAL 04

A name with presence.

A custom-drawn wordmark built around clear, compact capitals. One rising incision through the I suggests the first flash of ignition. The studio name leads; the game artwork carries the colour.

The ignition cut

The diagonal cut becomes a small identifying detail across the wordmark, favicon and transitions. It connects to Iskra’s meaning without another starburst or flame symbol.

A studio, across many worlds

Black, soft white and cool grey form a stable company identity. Game colours remain their own. The mark works in one colour, on artwork, on a loading screen or beside a publisher’s identifier.
