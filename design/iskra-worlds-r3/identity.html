04 / One-colour symbol

A quieter frame. Richer worlds.

Ink and warm white give Iskra a consistent home. Ember is a small signal, not a wall of orange. The games supply their own palettes: electric violet, misty green, deep blue—or whatever comes next.

Recognition through repetition.

A new symbol is not memorable by declaration. Its chance comes from a clear silhouette and consistent use: the same flints, central spark and opening movement across the website, game splash, trailers and small icons.

The ideas considered

01 / Strike — developed

Two flints create a central spark. A compact symbol with a repeatable opening gesture, connecting identity and motion.

02 / Ember — explored

A lasting flame rather than a burst. More organic, but the familiar fire silhouette could be harder to distinguish from other brands.

03 / Threshold — explored

A spark opening a window. Strong for a game directory, but its link to the Polish name is less direct than the flint concept.
