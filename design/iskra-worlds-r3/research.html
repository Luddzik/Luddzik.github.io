Pentagram / Nuverse ↗

The case study develops a gaming identity into a symbol, motion language and a modular family of visual elements. The lesson here is to derive related expressions from one idea. Iskra’s flints and opening sequence are an original proposal, not a reproduction of Nuverse’s forms.

Housemarque / Games ↗

A studio portfolio needs recognizable, distinct entries and a route to individual titles. Iskra’s directory uses independent artwork and metadata per game, with search and a growing grid.

Supergiant Games / Portfolio ↗

The studio’s games coexist under a stable studio identity. This supports keeping Iskra visually consistent while allowing each game’s world to bring its own palette and character.

Apple / AirPods Pro ↗

Reference for the user’s requested product-storytelling ambition. We reviewed the page’s content structure; its precise motion was not instrumented or copied. The Iskra prototype implements a comparable idea of staged reveal using original game assets and native scrolling.

WebKit / Scroll-driven animation ↗

Scroll position can act as the animation timeline. Iskra uses this principle in a small requestAnimationFrame implementation: scroll advances the world aperture, depth layers and gameplay reveal; scrolling back reverses them. The browser retains native scrolling.

How the system grows

Selected worlds are capped at three cinematic chapters. The complete collection is data-driven and has no three-game limit: the prototype exercises 1, 3, 8 and 24 entries. Search, filters and incremental display prevent a growing catalog from turning into an endless cinematic sequence.

Only NeuroShift is real. All other entries are explicitly labelled design specimens. No future Iskra project is named or implied. Reduced-motion mode removes pinning and uses static, readable sections.
